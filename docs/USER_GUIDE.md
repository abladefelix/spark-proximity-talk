# SKANAROUND — Using the app

SKANAROUND is a mobile app. Everything below happens on your phone; only the
admin console is meant for a desktop browser.

## 1. Create an account

1. Open the app → **Sign up**.
2. Enter a username, email, password, date of birth and gender.
   You must be 18 or older; younger dates of birth are rejected.
3. Confirm your email (or ask an admin to confirm it from Admin → Emails).
4. Sign back in with your **username or email** plus password.

Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen and follow
the emailed reset link.

## 2. Set up your profile

Profile tab:

- **Photo** — upload an avatar. Without one, a gender avatar is shown instead.
- **Display name, bio, gender**.
- **Scan range** — how far around you the radar looks, up to the maximum the
  admin allows.
- **Visibility** — go invisible to disappear from other people's radars.
- **Theme** — light or dark.
- **Radar tone** — optional sound when someone new appears; pick from the tones
  the admin uploaded.
- **Chat background** — wallpaper for your chat screens.
- **Verification** — submit a selfie to request the verified badge.
- **Delete account** — permanent, removes your profile, chats and data.

## 3. The radar

The radar is the home screen.

- Each dot is a person within your scan range. Distance from the centre is the
  real distance; direction is the real compass bearing.
- Tap **Enable compass** under the scope to switch to heading-up mode, so the
  radar rotates with the direction you are facing.
- Pinch or scroll to zoom; crowded beacons spread apart tangentially so you can
  still tap each one without losing their direction.
- Verified people have a distinct beacon colour; men and women get different
  gender avatars when they have no photo.
- Tap a beacon to open that person's profile card.

Location must be allowed ("While using the app" is enough). If you deny it, the
radar cannot show anyone.

## 4. Signalling and matching

1. Tap a beacon → **Signal** to show interest. Your username and photo are
   revealed to them.
2. They see an incoming signal card on their radar and can **Accept** or
   **Decline**.
3. When both people signal, it becomes a match and the chat opens.

Signals expire automatically (default 6 hours) and free accounts have a daily
signal limit set by the admin.

## 5. Chatting

- **Chats** tab shows a stacked deck of active chats, newest on top; tap to
  expand and pick a conversation.
- Send text, photos or a location pin. Photos upload to private storage and are
  only visible to the two of you.
- Long-press / use the header menu to **report** or **block** someone. Blocking
  removes them from your radar and ends the chat.
- Old chats are purged automatically after the retention window the admin sets.

## 6. Notifications

The bell icon shows admin announcements and activity alerts. On a real device
you also get push notifications for new signals, matches and messages.

## 7. Security lock

If biometric lock is enabled on your device, the app locks after ~20 seconds in
the background and requires Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint to reopen.

## 8. Go Pro

The **Go Pro** tab in the bottom bar appears when payments are enabled. Pro can
include unlimited signals, extended scan range, unlimited messages, seeing who
signalled you, a priority beacon and custom beacon styling — exactly which
perks are included is controlled by the admin.

Pay with Paystack (card or mobile money). After payment you get a branded
receipt by email and Pro activates immediately.
